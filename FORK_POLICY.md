# Project and Derivative-Work Policy

## Status

`Alexi5000/titanic-iceberg-run` is an **independent repository**, not a GitHub fork. It is maintained as Alexi5000’s applied reference implementation for browser-based visual systems, interaction engineering, and disciplined AI-assisted development.

## Purpose

The repository exists to make a complex client-side experience inspectable and reproducible. It brings together rendering, ocean and environmental effects, physics, input, gameplay state, local persistence, and UI without a backend or account system. Alexi5000 maintains it as a practical companion to more than a year of agent-building, integration, MCP-pattern, automation, Cursor, Claude, and Codex-class engineering work.

## Maintenance Boundaries

The maintainers prioritize changes that improve correctness, accessibility, performance, documentation, reproducibility, testability, asset governance, and maintainable project structure. They may decline changes that introduce avoidable backend complexity, credentials, telemetry, proprietary dependencies, unclear asset provenance, historical claims presented as fact, or broad visual rewrites without a validation plan.

## AI-Assisted Contributions

AI assistance is welcome when the contributor remains accountable for the change. Generated code, documentation, test fixtures, and assets must be reviewed, understood, attributed where required, and validated with the applicable quality commands. Automated tooling is not a substitute for source review, license review, or browser-level testing of visual changes.

## Attribution and Assets

The repository’s asset rules are described in [docs/ASSET_AND_LICENSES.md](docs/ASSET_AND_LICENSES.md). Do not add external imagery, models, textures, audio, trademarks, or references without documenting their source, license or permission basis, modification status, and any required attribution. Original procedural assets should remain traceable to their source modules.

## Derivative Work

You may create derivative work only under the rights and obligations of the repository’s applicable license and third-party asset terms. Preserve notices, avoid suggesting endorsement or affiliation, and clearly distinguish your changes. If you publish a derivative, link back to this repository where appropriate and include a meaningful description of your modifications.

## Upstream Changes

As this repository is independent, there is no upstream fork synchronization obligation. If external work informs a proposed change, identify the source in the pull request and ensure its license and attribution requirements are compatible before incorporation.
