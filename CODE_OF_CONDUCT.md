# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and participants are expected to engage professionally regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

Constructive technical disagreement, careful review, and evidence-based feedback are welcome. Communicate in good faith, respect differing perspectives, credit others’ work, and focus criticism on ideas or changes rather than people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, repeated bad-faith disruption, and any conduct that would make participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request revisions, remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and documented as appropriate for the situation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the support route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Provide relevant context, links, dates, and a safe contact method if possible. Reports will be handled discreetly and shared only with those who need to assess and resolve the concern.

Do **not** use this process for security vulnerabilities. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.
